According to the California Privacy Rights Act ("CPRA"), this policy describes the rights of California consumers of Visbody products regarding their personal information and Visbody's practices regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and sale of personal information as defined by the CPRA. When reviewing this policy, we further recommend that you review our other terms and conditions for more information about our overall privacy practices and your other rights under federal law. You agree to the following:

I. Your Rights:

  • a. Right to Know: This is your right to understand what categories of personal information we collect about you, how we use your personal information, whether we share, disclose, and/or sell your personal information to third parties, and other rights you may have regarding your personal information under the CPRA.
  • b. Right to Delete: This is your right to request that we delete personal information collected from you and stored in our systems, with certain exceptions that allow us to retain your personal information for specific purposes.
  • c. Right to Access: This is your right to request access to the personal information we have collected about you.
  • d. Right to Opt-Out of Sale: This is your right to opt out of having your personal information sold to third parties (if applicable).
  • e. Right to Equal Service: This is your right not to be retaliated against by Visbody in any way for exercising any rights under the CPRA or any other privacy policy agreement between you and Visbody.
  • f. Right to Correct: This is your right to request that Visbody correct inaccurate personal information you provided to Visbody, which you can reasonably prove is inaccurate.
  • g. Right to Limit Use: This is your right to limit Visbody's use of your personal information, as long as such limitation/restriction does not conflict with Visbody's rights and obligations under the law.
  • h. Right to Take Action: This is your right to bring a private legal action against Visbody for violations of the CPRA.

II. Categories of Personal Information Collected by Visbody Under the CPRA
In the past twelve months, Visbody may have collected the following categories of personal information, as defined by the CPRA, about California consumers (for more information about our collection practices, please review our Terms of Service):

  • Personal identifiers: Real name, alias, unique personal identifier, online identifier, Internet Protocol address, email address, account name, or other similar identifiers, including those personal information categories listed in the California Customer Records law, which also includes signature, physical characteristics or description, address, telephone number.
  • Biometric information: Genetic, physiological, behavioral, and biometric characteristics, or activity patterns used to extract templates or other identifiers or identifying information, such as facial marks, keystrokes, gait or other physical patterns, health or exercise data.
  • Internet or other similar network activity: Browsing history, search history, information on a consumer's interaction with a website, application, or advertisement.
  • Geolocation data: Physical location or movement.

III. Sources From Which We Collect Information Under the CPRA
Visbody collects certain personal information about you from various sources to provide our services, communicate with you and your service providers, and support your use of our services. These sources may include:

  • Information you provide directly to Visbody, such as when you contact or communicate with us, or when you establish a user account.
  • Information we automatically collect through your use of Visbody's services and products (including our websites and applications).
  • Information provided by third parties, such as your service providers.
  • Publicly available information about you.

IV. How Visbody Uses Your Information
For information on how Visbody uses the information we obtain from you, please review our Terms of Service.

V. Disclosure of Your Information
As described in Visbody's Terms of Service, Visbody does not sell your personal information to third parties for any purpose (including advertising) for monetary consideration. However, Visbody may share your personal information with the following entities for the following purposes:

A. Business Transfers:
In the process of a sale, merger, asset sale, or reorganization of Visbody or its affiliates, Visbody may disclose personal information. In such cases, your information will be transferred to the acquiring company. You will be provided with notice of such a transfer.

B. Third Parties:
Visbody has business relationships with third-party service providers. They help Visbody provide services to you, manage Visbody's business, and design, maintain, and improve Visbody's services, systems, programs, protocols, and security.
When we allow our contracted third-party service providers access to your personal information, they may only use it for purposes consistent with this policy. We ensure, through existing agreements, that these third parties have established levels of protection within their organizations equivalent to robust protection of your information. If our or our affiliates' business models undergo significant changes affecting the use of your information, an updated privacy policy will be provided.

C. Working with Service Providers and Business Partners:
Visbody works with other companies and individuals to provide services on behalf of Visbody. Any such subcontractors will comply with the provisions of 45 CFR § 164.502(b). Examples of service providers include data analytics companies, credit card usage companies, customer service and support providers, email and text message suppliers, web hosting and development companies, and fulfillment companies. These third parties may need access to your personal information to perform functions for Visbody, but use will be subject to contracts and agreements that protect the confidentiality of protected information.

D. Law Enforcement:
Visbody may disclose and report to law enforcement information about activities that Visbody reasonably believes to be unlawful, or information that Visbody reasonably believes may assist with law enforcement investigations of unlawful activity. In addition, Visbody reserves the right to release your information if Visbody determines, in its judgment, that releasing your information may help protect the safety or property of any person or entity.

E. Required or Permitted by Law:
Visbody may disclose your information to others as required or permitted by law. This may include disclosure of your information to government entities, or pursuant to court orders, subpoenas, warrants, summonses, or similar processes.

F. Protection of Visbody and Others:
Visbody may disclose information obtained from you when it believes it is necessary to investigate, prevent, or take action regarding illegal activities, suspected fraud, potential threats to the safety of any individual, violations of our Terms of Service or this policy, or as evidence in litigation in which Visbody is involved.

VI. Unrestricted Uses
The CPRA does not limit Visbody's ability to disclose the above personal information to third parties for previously stated purposes and the following purposes, including:

  • Compliance with federal, state, or local laws.
  • Compliance with civil, criminal, or regulatory inquiries, investigations, subpoenas, or summonses by federal, state, or local authorities.
  • Cooperation with law enforcement agencies concerning conduct or activity that Visbody, service providers, or third parties reasonably and in good faith believe may violate federal, state, or local laws.
  • Exercise or defense of legal claims.
  • Collection, use, retention, sale, or disclosure of deidentified or aggregated consumer information.
  • If all aspects of the collection or sale of the consumer's personal information takes place wholly outside of California.

VII. Your Right to Know About Your Personal Information
The CPRA requires that, upon request, Visbody must provide you with the following information:

  • The categories of personal information obtained from you.
  • The categories of sources from which the personal information was obtained.
  • The business and/or commercial purpose for collecting, disclosing, or selling personal information (if applicable).
  • The categories of third parties with whom Visbody shares your personal information.
  • The specific pieces of personal information obtained from you.

This is known as your "right to know" under the CPRA.

VIII. Your Right to Request Access to Your Personal Information
The CPRA requires Visbody to provide you with access to the actual personal information collected about you in the past twelve months; this is known as your "right of access" under the CPRA.
The CPRA requires Visbody to fulfill no more than two access requests within a twelve-month period, which are subject to limitations for manifestly unfounded or excessive requests.
Additionally, there are certain categories of personal information that Visbody may not return to you in response to your access request because the nature of that information is sensitive and may pose a substantial, articulable, and unreasonable risk to the security of that personal information.

IX. Your Right to Request Deletion of Your Personal Information
If you wish to request that Visbody delete your personal information, upon a verifiable consumer request, Visbody will delete your personal information from Visbody's systems, except where there are exceptions. Service providers and third parties will be notified that your personal information has been shared by Visbody.
However, in addition to some applicable terms and conditions requiring deletion of information, as found in Visbody's Terms of Service, your deletion request is subject to certain exceptions, which Visbody may rely on, where the company needs to retain personal information to:

  • Complete the transaction for which the personal information was collected, provide a product or service requested by you, or reasonably anticipated within the context of Visbody's ongoing business relationship with you, or otherwise perform a contract between Visbody and you.
  • Detect security incidents, protect against malicious, fraudulent, illegal activity, or prosecute those responsible for such activities.
  • Debug to identify and repair errors that impair existing intended functionality.
  • Exercise free speech, ensure the right of another consumer to exercise their right to free speech, or exercise another right provided for by law.
  • Comply with the California Electronic Communications Privacy Act, as applicable.
  • Engage in public or peer-reviewed scientific, historical, or statistical research in the public interest that adheres to all other applicable ethics and privacy laws, when the deletion of information may render impossible or seriously impair the achievement of such research, if you have provided informed consent.
  • Enable solely internal uses that are reasonably aligned with consumer expectations based on the consumer's relationship with Visbody.
  • Comply with a legal obligation.
  • Otherwise use the personal information, internally, in a lawful manner that is compatible with the context in which you provided the information.

If any of these exceptions apply, Visbody need not delete your personal information. If your request is denied because of any of these exceptions, Visbody will notify you in writing of the reason.

X. Submitting Requests
Given the sensitivity of information under the CPRA and the provisions of the law itself, Visbody must verify your identity when using your request, whether for access to or deletion of your personal information. While the verification process involves using personal information associated with you as the requester, Visbody will not collect or retain information you provide during the request process, provided that Visbody will retain contact information provided to fulfill your request or otherwise communicate with you regarding your request. If Visbody cannot verify your identity, your request will be denied.
To exercise any of your rights under the CPRA, please contact us by sending an email to LBSupport@Visbody.com or contacting us.

XI. Your Right to Equal Service
The CPRA prohibits businesses from discriminating against California consumers for exercising any of their rights under the CPRA. This means that if you exercise any rights under this policy in the CPRA, Visbody may not:

  • Deny goods or services to you;
  • Charge you different prices for goods or services than you would otherwise be charged; or
  • Provide you with a different quality level of goods or services;

For information about Visbody's general privacy practices (some of which may apply to you, some of which may not), please visit Visbody's privacy policy page.

XIII. Updates
